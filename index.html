Greg Slabaugh

Professor, and Director of the Digital Environment Research Institute (DERI)

Queen Mary University of London



Gregory G. Slabaugh is Professor of Computer Vision and AI and Director of the Digital Environment Research Institute (DERI) at Queen Mary University of London. He is also Academic Turing Liaison for Queen Mary at The Alan Turing Institute. He earned a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, USA. Previously, he was Chief Scientist in Computer Vision (EU) for Huawei Technologies R&D, and other prior appointments include City, University of London, Medicsight, and Siemens. He holds 49 granted patent families and has over 300 per-reviewed publications. He regularly serves as Area Chair in computer vision conferences (CVPR, ECCV) and on the technical program committee for numerous AI and computer vision conferences.
